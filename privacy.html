Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

PinAI is a Pinterest business management and content workflow application currently in development.

Information we may process

Depending on the features used, PinAI may process information you enter into the application, such as trends, product information, content drafts, Pins, and performance data.

Pinterest authorization

If Pinterest integration is enabled, PinAI will request only the Pinterest permissions needed for the enabled functionality. Pinterest authorization is performed through Pinterest's official authorization flow. PinAI does not ask users to provide their Pinterest password to PinAI.

Local development

The current MVP is designed as a local-first application. Data may be stored locally on the user's device during development.

Third-party services

When a third-party service is connected, information may be processed according to that service's own privacy policy and the permissions granted by the user.

Security

Authentication credentials, application secrets, and access tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed in client-side code.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PinAI operator through the contact method associated with the application.
